GRR vs. Growth ARR vs. NRR.

All three have "recurring revenue" in the name. They measure different things. Here's the mental model.

Mental model
Read before the scorecard
THE BUCKET
Your book is a bucket of water. GRR is how much stayed in. Growth ARR is new water poured on top. Divide the final level by the starting level → that's NRR.
START OF PERIOD END OF PERIOD
$500K starting ARR $420K kept + $195K added = $615K · NRR 123%
GRR
Gross Recurring Revenue
Retention only. What you kept of what you started with.
CEILING · 100%
ARRgrowth
Growth Annual Recurring Revenue
Expansion only. New dollars on top — upsell, cross-sell, uplift.
NO CEILING · $ TARGET
NRR
Net Recurring Revenue
Retention + expansion, combined. Booked ÷ starting ARR.
BEST REPS · 110%+
WHY GIANNA TRACKS THEM SEPARATELY
Two reps can hit the same NRR — with very different book health.
✓ HEALTHY
Flat renewals plus strong expansion. The book is solid underneath the growth.
GRR 100% · Growth big → NRR 115%
✗ MASKED
Some churn, covered by massive expansion. The NRR looks fine; the retention doesn't.
GRR 85% · Growth huge → NRR 115%
